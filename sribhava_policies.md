## POLICY: Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an order free of charge within 60 minutes of placing it, provided the order has not yet entered the "Processing" stage in our fulfilment system. Once an order has moved to "Processing," cancellation is no longer guaranteed, but customers may request cancellation and our fulfilment team will attempt to intercept the shipment. If interception is unsuccessful, the order will be treated as a standard return once delivered.

Cancellation requests must be submitted through the account dashboard or via a support ticket. No cancellation fee applies within the 60-minute window. After this window, if the order has already shipped, standard return policy applies instead.

**Escalation rule:** Any cancellation request involving an order value over $500 must be escalated to a human reviewer before being confirmed, regardless of timing.

## POLICY: Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of an approved return or cancellation. Approved refund reasons include: item not as described, item damaged in transit, order not received within the guaranteed delivery window, and duplicate charges.

Refunds for change-of-mind returns are only available within 30 days of delivery, and the item must be returned in original, unused condition. A restocking fee of 10% applies to change-of-mind returns on items over $200.

**Refund amount thresholds:**
- Under $50, with an approved reason: may be auto-processed without human review.
- $50 to $300, with an approved reason: may be auto-processed without human review, provided the reason is clearly one of the approved categories listed above.
- Over $300, or any refund citing a reason not on the approved list: must be escalated to a human reviewer, regardless of the amount.

**Escalation rule:** Any refund request exceeding $300, or citing a reason other than the approved list above, must be escalated to a human reviewer.

## POLICY: Account Access and Security

Customers who report being unable to access their account should first be guided through the standard password reset flow via the account recovery page. If a customer reports suspicious activity on their account (unrecognised orders, unrecognised login locations), their account must be temporarily locked and the request escalated to a human reviewer immediately — this is a mandatory escalation regardless of any other factors.

Requests to change the email address associated with an account require identity verification (order history confirmation or billing address match) before the change is processed.

**Escalation rule:** Any report of suspected unauthorised account access is always escalated, with no auto-resolution path.

## POLICY: Shipping

Standard shipping typically takes 3–5 business days within metropolitan areas, and 5–8 business days for regional and remote areas. Express shipping, where selected at checkout, typically delivers within 1–2 business days. Shipping method cannot be changed once an order has entered the "Processing" stage.

**Escalation rule:** Requests to change a delivery address after an order has already shipped must be escalated to a human reviewer, since this requires direct carrier coordination.

---

## POLICY: Delivery

If a customer reports non-receipt of an order past the guaranteed delivery window (standard: 10 business days from dispatch; express: 4 business days from dispatch), a replacement or refund may be offered once a support agent has confirmed with the carrier that the shipment is confirmed lost.

Orders under $100 that are past the guaranteed window may be auto-resolved with a replacement shipment. Orders over $100 require human confirmation of carrier loss before any resolution.

**Escalation rule:** Any non-receipt claim on an order over $100, or any claim where the carrier has not confirmed loss, must be escalated to a human reviewer.

---

## POLICY: Payment and Billing

Accepted payment methods are credit/debit card, PayPal, and bank transfer. If a customer reports being charged incorrectly (wrong amount, duplicate charge, or a charge after order cancellation), this is treated as a billing dispute.

Billing disputes under $50 may be auto-resolved with a refund of the disputed amount once the duplicate or incorrect charge is confirmed in our payment system logs. Billing disputes of $50 or more must always be escalated to a human reviewer, along with any dispute where the payment system logs do not clearly confirm the customer's claim.

---

## POLICY: Escalation Summary

The following situations always require human review, regardless of dollar amount:
- Suspected unauthorised account access
- Any refund reason not on the approved list
- Any cancellation on an order over $500
- Any billing dispute of $50 or more, or one not clearly confirmed by system logs
- Any customer request the system cannot confidently classify into an existing category

## POLICY: Subscription and Membership Management

Customers on a recurring subscription plan may cancel at any time through the account dashboard, effective at the end of the current billing cycle — no partial refunds are issued for the remaining days of an already-paid cycle. Customers may pause a subscription for up to 3 months per calendar year without losing their membership tier or accumulated loyalty benefits.

Requests to downgrade a subscription tier take effect at the next billing cycle; upgrades take effect immediately, with a prorated charge for the remainder of the current cycle.

**Escalation rule:** Any request to cancel and receive a refund for the current billing cycle (rather than cancelling at cycle-end) must be escalated to a human reviewer.

## POLICY: Invoices and Billing Statements

Customers may request a copy of any invoice from the past 24 months directly through the account dashboard's Billing History section. Invoices older than 24 months require a manual request, processed within 3 business days.

If a customer disputes a line item on an invoice (incorrect tax calculation, incorrect item price), the disputed line item must be verified against the original order record before any correction is issued.

**Escalation rule:** Any invoice dispute that cannot be resolved by direct comparison to the original order record must be escalated to a human reviewer.

---

## POLICY: Product Warranty and Defects

Products carry a standard 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of delivery. A defect claim requires photographic evidence of the issue, submitted through the support ticket. Warranty claims resulting in a replacement are shipped via standard shipping at no charge to the customer.

Damage caused by misuse, accidents, or normal wear and tear is not covered under warranty and will be assessed on a case-by-case basis.

**Escalation rule:** Any warranty claim where misuse versus manufacturing defect is unclear from the submitted evidence must be escalated to a human reviewer for manual assessment.

---

## POLICY: Complaints and Feedback

General feedback and product suggestions submitted through support tickets should be acknowledged and logged, with no further action required beyond a thank-you response. Formal complaints — meaning a customer explicitly expressing dissatisfaction with service received, requesting compensation, or threatening to escalate publicly (social media, review platforms) — are treated differently from general feedback.

**Escalation rule:** Any formal complaint, any request for compensation beyond a standard refund or replacement, and any mention of public escalation (social media, review platforms, regulatory bodies) must always be escalated to a human reviewer.

---

## POLICY: Contact and Support Channels

Customers can reach support through three channels: the in-app live chat (available 24/7 for account and order queries), email support (typical response time 1 business day), and phone support (available Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm local time, for urgent account or payment issues only).

General product questions and order status queries should be directed to live chat or email first, as these are handled fastest through automated and semi-automated triage. Phone support is reserved for issues involving account lockouts, suspected fraud, or payment disputes requiring identity verification.

**Escalation rule:** Any contact request specifically asking to speak with a manager or supervisor, or any customer indicating this is a repeat contact about an unresolved issue, must be escalated to a human reviewer.
